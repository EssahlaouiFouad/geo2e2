About us

A consultancy serving your projects.

GEO2E2 is an approved consultancy that supports its partners in developing mining projects as well as projects related to water resources and the environment. It also provides professional training in various geoscience disciplines.

Our organisation places in-depth expertise and a resolutely innovative approach at the service of its partners. Backed by a team of highly qualified experts and a rigorous methodology, we design and deploy tailor-made solutions suited to the specific characteristics of each project.

Our ambition is to provide reliable, sustainable and high-performing answers to today's challenges in the mining sector and in water resource management.

  • In-depth expertise and a multidisciplinary team
  • A rigorous methodology, from fieldwork to deliverable
  • Tailor-made, sustainable and cost-effective solutions

Tools & methodology

From fieldwork to deliverable, a controlled process.

Our methodology is based primarily on fieldwork, laboratory testing and analysis, and the processing of a comprehensive geological and geographical database. The data is compiled, analysed and interpreted to produce controlled modelling and design.

  1. 01

    Fieldwork & data collection

    Field surveys, sampling and the siting of boreholes.

  2. 02

    Analysis & laboratory

    Testing, analysis and characterisation of the samples collected.

  3. 03

    Processing & interpretation

    Geophysics, geological data, GIS, remote sensing and spatial modelling.

  4. 04

    Control & deliverables

    Validation of results, reserve estimation and final technical documents.
