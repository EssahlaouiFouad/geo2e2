Tools & methodology

A rigorous process combining fieldwork, laboratory analysis, data processing and modelling to deliver reliable, fit-for-purpose results.

Our working methodology

From fieldwork to decision-making, with rigour and method.

While we adopt solutions tailored to the requirements of each project, our methodology is based primarily on fieldwork, laboratory testing and analysis, and the processing of a comprehensive database. This data is then compiled, analysed and interpreted to produce modelling and design that are controlled through to the final deliverables.

  1. 01

    Fieldwork & data collection

    Field surveys, sampling and the siting of exploration and production boreholes.

  2. 02

    Analysis & laboratory

    Testing and analysis of samples to characterise materials and resources.

  3. 03

    Processing & interpretation

    Database compilation, geophysical processing, spatial analysis and interpretation.

  4. 04

    Modelling & design

    Spatial modelling, reserve estimation and integration of AI approaches where relevant.

  5. 05

    Control & deliverables

    Validation of results and production of clear, usable technical documents.
